Send both access emails to the buyer with distinct passwords; label the second email for forwarding. Fulfill both orders atomically from the same verified Stripe Session, avoid duplicates, and apply a full payment refund to both orders.

Fulfill only verified paid Stripe Checkout Sessions or explicitly approved `free` voucher orders with a completed, server-verified zero-cost Stripe Session; use signed webhooks and idempotent order creation. Never grant purchases from URL parameters or browser storage. Never enable live checkout before Stripe and the email sender are configured and verified.
